\section{Master Index}
\label{sec:master-index}

\prerequisite{
None. This section is intended as a navigation reference
for the complete textbook.
}

\learningobjectives{
By the end of this reference section, the reader should be able to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item locate major mathematical subjects in the textbook;
    \item identify relationships among mathematical disciplines;
    \item distinguish foundational, pure, applied, and computational topics;
    \item navigate from elementary prerequisites to advanced subjects;
    \item use the textbook as an interconnected mathematical reference.
\end{itemize}
}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{How to Use the Master Index}
\label{subsec:master-index-use}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

This index organizes major topics alphabetically and directs the reader to
their primary location in the textbook.

A topic may appear in several chapters.

The listed location identifies its principal treatment or the most useful
starting point.

    connects Statistics, Stochastic Processes, Finance, Biology,
    Queueing, and Statistical Mechanics.

    connects Combinatorics, Algorithms, Optimization, Networks,
    Biology, and Social Systems.

    connects Geometry, Topology, Mechanics, Relativity,
    and Geometric Analysis.

    connects Algebra, Geometry, Complex Analysis, and Cryptography.

\end{itemize}

\chapterconnection{
The Master Index completes the mathematical reference chapter and provides
navigation across the full structure of \emph{The Composition of Advanced
Mathematics}. Mathematics is not a collection of isolated subjects: algebra
provides structure, analysis describes limits and change, geometry describes
space, topology describes continuity and form, probability describes
uncertainty, statistics extracts information from data, differential
equations describe evolving systems, optimization identifies preferred
solutions, computation makes complex mathematics operational, logic provides
formal foundations, and applied mathematics combines these ideas to model the
world. The index is therefore intended not only to locate topics, but also to
make their relationships visible.
